# BEFOR9 for Organizations — Pilot Program

## Positioning
BEFOR9 is a proposed managed internship and graduate talent development partner for employers, banks and large organizations. We coordinate intern readiness, structured learning, progress tracking, supervisor feedback and evidence-based talent pipelines.

This is a **proposed service**, not a claim of existing clients, accreditations, outcomes or partnerships.

## Problem
- Interns can start without a clear training plan, equipment or approved access.
- Supervisors may have limited capacity to coach and document progress.
- HR may lack consistent, comparable evidence of attendance, learning and performance.
- Potential future hires may be overlooked despite strong performance.

## Five service pillars
1. **Preboarding readiness** — track device and access requests, owner, due date and escalation; employer IT approves and provisions all access.
2. **Foundation week** — an employer-approved introductory week focused on workplace norms, security awareness, communication and practical skills.
3. **Department placement** — agreed learning goals and supervised assignments in the host department, with a proposed daily one-hour BEFOR9 session subject to operational approval.
4. **Progress reporting** — attendance and learning evidence, supervisor feedback, support needs, and monthly HR summaries with transparent rubrics.
5. **Talent pipeline** — identify graduates who may match real open roles; final hiring, compensation and suitability decisions remain with the employer.

## Pilot proposal
- One employer, one cohort (e.g. 10–20 interns), one defined internship cycle
- HR sponsor + department supervisors + IT coordinator + BEFOR9 program lead
- Baseline readiness checklist, weekly status, monthly dashboard and final review
- Success measures: day-one readiness, time-to-access, attendance, course completion, supervisor feedback, and internship-to-interview conversion
- Pilot scope, commercial terms, onsite delivery, liability and data-processing agreements to be agreed before launch

## Sample weekly timetable
- **Week 1:** structured induction and foundation learning, with employer-specific mandatory orientation and security sessions
- **Weeks 2 onward:** supervised department work plus up to one hour of structured learning/check-in daily, adjusted with managers
- **Weekly:** supervisor check-in and issue escalation
- **Monthly:** HR review and cohort report
- **Final week:** skill evidence, intern feedback, final assessment and potential role matching

## Reporting and fairness
- Use predefined job-related criteria; separate verified facts from manager opinions.
- Share performance reports only with authorized HR and relevant managers, with appropriate notices and lawful basis.
- Minimize personal data, set retention periods and access controls; comply with applicable Saudi privacy, employment and cybersecurity requirements.
- Avoid promising guaranteed employment, guaranteed device/access provisioning or guaranteed cost savings.
- Any salary comparisons must compare role requirements and total employment cost fairly; a junior graduate is not a like-for-like replacement for an experienced specialist.
